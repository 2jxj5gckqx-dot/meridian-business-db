## ITEM 29 – WFDB Inner Rules / By-Laws: non-compliance with award -> suspension/expulsion across affiliated bourses; can a non-member bring a claim at IDE arbitration?

### 29(a) WFDB rule on non-compliance with an arbitration award
Status: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (mechanism exists, but it is NOT an automatic "expulsion from all bourses", and – critically – the IDE left the WFDB in April 2025, see 29(c)).

Correct information:
- The governing document is "WORLD FEDERATION OF DIAMOND BOURSES BY-LAWS AND INNER RULES, September 2020" (PDF on wfdb.com, uploaded July 2021). Primary source.
- Per the search-engine excerpts of that PDF, the by-laws list grounds on which a bourse must report a member to the WFDB, including "Not complying with a final arbitration judgment", failure to deposit arbitration security, and bankruptcy of a bourse member; the reporting bourse must communicate the facts to the Secretary-General, who transmits them to all affiliated bourses, which are then "compelled to take appropriate disciplinary action" under their OWN constitution/by-laws/inner rules and national law.
- So the mechanism is: report -> Secretary-General -> circulation to all affiliated bourses -> each bourse applies its own disciplinary procedure. It is a reciprocal-notification/discipline duty, not a single act of expulsion from all bourses.

Exact quotes [snippet-verbatim, from search excerpts of the PDF]:
- "Not complying with a final arbitration judgment" (listed as a ground for disciplinary reporting).
- "Such facts shall immediately be communicated by the relevant bourse to the Secretary-General, who shall transmit the information to the affiliated bourses."
- "...affiliated bourses are compelled to take appropriate disciplinary action in terms of the procedures prescribed in their own Constitution or By-Laws and Inner Rules as well as national law..."
- WFDB object clause (excerpt): the WFDB has the object of "furthering the amicable settlement or arbitration of differences and disputes between the individual members of the affiliated bourses and between the affiliated bourses".
- Inner Rules Art. 4 Sec. A as quoted in a New York court decision (Elul Diamonds Co. v. Z Kor Diamonds, N.Y. Sup. Ct., index 0115640/2006, decided 6 June 2007): "[a]ny member of a Bourse affiliated to the WFDB shall submit to exclusive adjudication of a dispute involving a member of different Bourse affiliated to the WFDB, as set out hereunder".
- Secondary description (academia.edu paper "The Diamond Industry and the Industry's Dispute Resolution Mechanisms", re the New York DDC): "The winning party may seek to have the losing party suspended from the DDC which, if approved by the Club's Board of Directors, would lead to that person being barred from all of the Club's affiliated with the World Federation of Diamond Bourses."

### 29(b) Can a NON-member (e.g. a lender) bring a claim at IDE arbitration against a member who signed an arbitration undertaking?
Status: PARTIALLY CONFIRMED (yes in principle, on the basis of a specific WRITTEN undertaking; the IDE bylaws themselves could not be read).

Correct information:
- Israeli Arbitration Law, 5728-1968, s.1 defines an arbitration agreement as a written agreement between the parties; there is no membership requirement in the statute – any two parties (member or not) can agree in writing to arbitrate before the IDE arbitration institute. Verbatim statutory text as returned by search (matches the statute): "הסכם בוררות" – "הסכם בכתב למסור לבוררות סכסוך שנתגלע בין צדדים להסכם או שעשוי להתגלע ביניהם בעתיד, בין שנקוב בהסכם שמו של בורר ובין אם לאו". URL: https://www.nevo.co.il/law_html/law00/74417.htm (primary; also https://he.wikisource.org/wiki/חוק_הבוררות).
- Israeli court decisions summarised on dinrega.com (secondary summaries of primary judgments; case numbers not retrievable because pages were blocked) state that the IDE arbitration institute's jurisdiction covers the categories that accept the bylaws (members, conditional members, trainees, tenants) AND extends to persons/entities outside those categories who agreed in a specific written undertaking. Search-excerpt wording (English rendering by the search tool of the Hebrew page): "The arbitration institution has authority to decide disputes between members listed in the categories recognized by the regulations, and this authority also extends to those not included in these categories ('a person or other legal entity') who have agreed to accept its authority through a specific written undertaking or special instruction to accept the authority of the arbitration institution." and "Non-members who are not 'a member, conditional member, trainee and/or tenant of the Diamond Exchange' can consent to the arbitration institution's jurisdiction by signing a written undertaking..." (The Hebrew phrase quoted in the excerpt is "אדם או גוף משפטי אחר".) Another dinrega summary: "even though one party was not a member of the diamond exchange, they agreed to arbitration before the arbitration institution of the diamond exchange. The district court ruled that there was no basis for the claim about lack of authority." URLs: https://www.dinrega.com/gs/סמכות מוסד הבוררות של בורסת היהלומים ; https://www.dinrega.com/gs/מוסד הבוררות של הבורסה ליהלומים מוסמך ; https://www.dinrega.com/gs/סמכות מוסד הבוררות של בורסת היהלומים בסכסוך משפחתי (secondary).
- Supreme Court, רע"א 1307/18 אלן ספירא נ' יורגן ביק (Justice D. Mintz): per the Afik & Co. summary, on 6 July 2010 the applicant signed an undertaking towards the Israel Diamond Exchange (1965) Ltd. as part of a lease of premises; the District Court held the undertaking constituted a valid arbitration agreement between the applicant and the respondent even though it was signed in the framework of a lease; leave to appeal was dealt with by the Supreme Court. URL: https://he.afiklaw.com/caselaw/6838 (secondary summary of a primary decision). Nevo copy not retrievable.
- Counter-example (2025, Tel Aviv District Court, exact case number not retrieved): the "exploded million-shekel diamond" case (employee Netanel/Natan Yelizarov of "The Diamonds O.Y. Diamonds & Jewelry Ltd."): press reports state that a company that is NOT a member is NOT subject to the IDE arbitration institute, so the suppliers sued the employee (a member) personally. Search-excerpt wording: "The Israeli diamond exchange is a closed organization of diamond traders whose members are bound by internal arbitration procedures in case of disputes, whereas those who are not members are not subject to these procedures... the company - despite its official license to trade diamonds - is not subject to the arbitration institution." The IDE legal committee rejected the employee's jurisdiction objection; a motion to the Tel Aviv District Court was filed (outcome not found). URLs: https://www.mako.co.il/news-israel/2025_q2/Article-95e089104ca3691027.htm ; https://ch10.co.il/news/976602/ ; https://www.srugim.co.il/1116614-... ; https://www.ice.co.il/finance/news/article/1060191 (all secondary, 2025 Q2).
- Implication for VAT Bridge: a non-member lender cannot rely on the client's IDE membership alone; it must have the client sign a specific written arbitration undertaking naming the IDE arbitration institute (and the institute must accept the file). Without that, an award binds only members and disputes with non-member counterparties go to the ordinary courts (isde.co.il terms note that outside the arbitration agreement exclusive jurisdiction lies with the Tel Aviv/Central district courts – search excerpt of https://www.isde.co.il/article.aspx?id=21841).
Source type: primary (statute) / secondary (case summaries, press).

### 29(c) CRITICAL NEW FACT – IDE is no longer a WFDB member
Status: CONFIRMED (secondary sources; multiple independent trade outlets), no evidence found of re-admission as of 2026-09-17.
Correct information: In April 2025 the IDE board unanimously "suspended" its WFDB membership amid a dispute over the WFDB's response to the US tariff crisis (IDE President Nissim Zuaretz vs WFDB President Yoram Dvash). The WFDB treated the letter as a resignation and ruled that the IDE is no longer a member; going forward IDE members cannot use the WFDB worldwide arbitration system and the IDE cannot call itself a WFDB-affiliated bourse. Search-excerpt wording: "The WFDB ruled that the Israel Diamond Exchange is no longer a member, stipulating that, going forward, IDE members cannot engage in the global body's worldwide arbitration system and the IDE cannot label itself a WFDB-affiliated bourse." and "In its reply, the WFDB noted that only it can suspend or expel members, so it considered the IDE's letter a 'resignation notice.'" A search for re-joining/reinstatement in 2026 returned nothing. (Related: Zuaretz resigned as IDE president in 2026 per ynet, amid record-low exports – secondary.)
URLs: https://rapaport.com/news/israel-diamond-exchange-quits-wfdb-amid-tariff-row/ ; https://www.jckonline.com/editorial-article/israeli-bourse-leaves-wfdb/ ; https://gjepc.org/solitaire/israel-diamond-exchange-quits-wfdb-amid-tariff-dispute/ ; https://www.thediamondpress.com/post/april-2025-news-roundup (all secondary).

## BOTTOM LINE FOR THE DECK
1. "IDE arbitration is an enforcement mechanism" – partially true: awards are enforceable via court confirmation under the Arbitration Law and the IDE can discipline its own members; but a non-member lender must obtain a specific signed arbitration undertaking from the client, otherwise it has no standing before the institute (2025 press cases show non-member companies are outside the institute's jurisdiction).
2. "Non-payment -> blacklisting in all WFDB bourses worldwide" – should be removed or heavily qualified: the WFDB rule is a report-and-circulate mechanism with discipline left to each bourse, AND the IDE has not been a WFDB member since April 2025 (WFDB: IDE members "cannot engage in the global body's worldwide arbitration system").
3. All quotes above come from search-engine excerpts, not fetched pages – re-verify against the live wfdb.com PDF and isde.co.il before publication.
