What is VAT Bridge?

An advance on the VAT refund the state owes your business, provided by MDPS.

What do I need?

Your latest VAT return and your business details.

Who is it for?

Businesses registered for VAT in Israel whose return shows a refund due. Typically exporters and importers: they pay VAT on purchases and at customs (18%), and charge little or no VAT on sales, because exports are zero-rated.

What does it cost?

It depends on the amount and on when the state pays. You receive the terms in writing before you sign anything, and nothing is due before that.

What if the state audits my return?

Then the state may take up to 90 days instead of 30. That wait is exactly what VAT Bridge is there for. If the refund is delayed or reduced, your agreement sets out what happens.

Does it change my VAT reporting?

No. You keep filing as usual.
