Accessibility statement

We want everyone to be able to use this site, including people with disabilities. This statement is made under the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities Law, 5758-1998, and the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013.

Before going live. Add the accessibility coordinator's details [to be completed by MDPS] and, if MDPS's offices receive the public, the physical-accessibility arrangements [to be completed by MDPS].

What we did

  • The site targets Israeli Standard IS 5568 (WCAG 2.0 level AA).
  • All text is real text, resizable with the browser's zoom, with contrast at or above AA.
  • Everything works with a keyboard: links, buttons, the form and the interactive amount field have visible focus.
  • Animations respect the operating system's "reduce motion" setting and are then switched off.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.
  • Form fields have labels, and errors are announced in text.
  • The site works on phones, tablets and desktops, in portrait and landscape.

Known limits

The scroll-driven animation on the "Filed on…" screen is illustrative; its content (the dates and the day count) is also given in text. If you find something that does not work for you, tell us and we will fix it.
