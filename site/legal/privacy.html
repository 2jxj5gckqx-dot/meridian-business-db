Privacy policy

This page explains what information this website collects, why, and what your rights are. It is written to comply with the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and its regulations.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of the information is MDPS [to be completed by MDPS] (legal name, company number, registered address), part of the Mazalit group. Contact for privacy matters: [to be completed by MDPS] (email).

2. What we collect

Only what you type into the request form: business name, company number, contact name, phone, email and, if you choose to give it, the refund amount on your VAT return. You are not legally required to provide this information; without it, we cannot process a request.

Hosting logs: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may record technical data such as IP address and browser type in its server logs, under GitHub's privacy statement.

3. What we do not collect

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising trackers. The amount you type at the top of the page and in the example VAT return stays in your browser and is not sent anywhere unless you submit the form.

4. Why we use it

To review your request for an advance on your VAT refund, to contact you about it, and to meet legal obligations that apply to a licensed credit provider. We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing unless you agree separately.
